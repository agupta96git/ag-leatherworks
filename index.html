Questions
How long does an order take?

Each piece is made to order. Lead time is 1–3 weeks from confirmation; cardholders are quickest.

How does payment work?

A 50% deposit starts your piece; the balance is due at pickup or delivery. I accept PayPal, Venmo and Zelle.

Can I choose the leather and thread?

Yes. Choose each part in the designer. I confirm thread options by email before I cut. Locals can see swatches in person.

Do you ship?

Arlington and nearby Northern Virginia customers pick up free. Shipping is available nationwide at cost.

Can you make something not in the collection?

Happy to talk it through. Email me what you have in mind.

Do you take corporate or bulk orders?

Yes: gifts for your team, client events or executive recognition. Bulk pricing is available.
